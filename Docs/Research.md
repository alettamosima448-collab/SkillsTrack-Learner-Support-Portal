# Learner Support Portal - Research

## 1. Introduction

The Learner Support Portal is a web application designed to support learners by helping them manage their learning tasks, support bookings and learning progress.

The purpose of this research is to understand the needs of learners and identify the types of features that can make the portal useful and easy to use.

## 2. Purpose of the Research 

The research aims to understand the challenges learners may experience when managing their learning responsibilities and accessing support.

The information gathered from the research will help the development team make informed decisions about the requirements, features and design of the Learner Support Portal.

## 3. Research Objectives

The objectives of the research are to:

- Identify the needs of learners using the portal.
- Understand how learners manage their learning tasks.
- Identify ways learners can access and manage support bookings.
- Identify useful ways for learners to monitor their learning progress.
- Identify features that could make the portal simple and easy to use.
- Provide information that can guide the development of the system.

## 4. Target Users

The main target users of the Learner Support Portal are learners who require assistance with managing their learning tasks, accessing support and monitoring their progress.

## 5. Research Questions

The following questions will guide the research:

1. What challenges do learners experience when managing their learning tasks?
2. What types of support do learners need?
3. How can learners access support more easily?
4. How can a portal help learners manage their support bookings?
5. What information would learners need to monitor their progress?
6. What features would make the portal easy to use?
7. What problems should the Learner Support Portal aim to solve?

## 6. Proposed Research Methods

The team can gather information using methods such as:

- Questionnaires or surveys with learners.
- Interviews with learners.
- Research into existing learner support systems.
- Review of the project brief and requirements.

The results of the research can be used to identify common learner needs and inform the design and development of the system.
